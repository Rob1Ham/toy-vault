# Toy Vault — rules card (no-code track)

Take one rule. Write a cheat script. That's the exercise.

## The five rules

1. **Only the owner** — or someone the owner named — may hand a spend request to the vault.
2. **Two different approved people** must say yes. The same name twice is one person.
3. **The vault can never go below zero.** The fee counts as part of the spend.
4. **The same request must never pay twice.**
5. **The preview and the real spend must agree.**

## Your cheat script (fill this in)

- **Who I am:** (the owner? a stranger? an approved helper? the same helper twice?)
- **What I send:** (amount, fee, destination, how many approvals, and whose names)
- **What I expect to happen:** (what the vault does if the flaw is real)
- **What the rules say should happen:** (which rule, in your own words)
- **What I did NOT check:** (be honest — one thing is enough)

## Example (yours should be different)

- **Who I am:** a total stranger at the counter.
- **What I send:** a normal-looking spend with two real approver names I copied from a memo.
- **What I expect:** the vault pays out even though I'm nobody.
- **The rule:** rule 1 — only the owner or their delegate submits.
- **Not checked:** whether the vault keeps a record of who submitted.

## Vocabulary (optional)

- **Trust boundary** — the door. Anything past it was checked by someone; anything at it must be checked now.
- **Invariant** — a rule that must stay true no matter what, like never-below-zero.
- **Hunch vs. finding** — a hunch is a story; a finding is a story you safely made happen and can also make stop.

Remember: fake names, fake money, this folder only.
